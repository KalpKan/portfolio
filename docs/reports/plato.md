### D21 — A Registrar-scheduled final is dated on the first day of the exam period when a schedule row or range sits next to it

Severity: **blocker** (story 4 in one sentence: "the `.ics` never contains an invented date"; 2 of 42 live calendars carry one, and the review page shows it as a plain date with no "needs a date" badge and no reason, so the student has no cue)

Steps to reproduce: upload `B3415G Course Outline 2025.pdf` (tick re-read), read the Final Exam row; download. Or `2242A001.pdf` (MOS 2242A), Exam 3.

Expected / Actual: Biol 3415G Final Exam "Needs a date — Outline says: scheduled by the Registrar (exam period Apr 7 – Apr 30, 2025)", no event. Actual: "Final Exam · 40.0% · Apr 07, 2025", `SUMMARY:Biol 3415G: Final Exam due` `DTSTART;TZID=America/Toronto:20250407T235900` plus a `start: Final Exam` study event (`plato-r3-biol3415g-live-2026-09-19.ics`, `plato-r3-review-b3415g-1280-2026-09-19.jpg`). Likely cause: two paths. (a) `src/outline/assessments.py:385–411` `_enrich_from_schedule` skips rows whose status is `exact`/`recurring`/`rule` but **not `registrar`/`range`/`tba`**, so the evaluation table's correct `registrar` result is overwritten by the week-by-week schedule row "April 7-30 | April exam period | Final Exam scheduled by the registrar" (its date cell "April 7-30" resolves to `exact` Apr 7, the title matches at 0.6, and only "TBA/TBD" rows are excluded at line 393, not "registrar"/"exam period"). (b) `src/outline/dates.py:271–286`: `_find_dates` returns Dec 11 from "Dec. 11-22, 2025", so `exam_mention and not dates` is false and the row falls through to `exact` with the first date instead of `range`.

Suggested fix: in `_enrich_from_schedule`, skip items whose status is already `registrar`/`range`/`tba` and skip schedule rows whose text matches `registrar|exam period|to be announced`; in `dates.py`, treat "<Month> d-d" / "d–d" as a window (status `range`) rather than a date, and never let an `exam_mention`/`registrar` sentence produce `exact`; make the scorer's `no_fabricated` count a `registrar` item as fabricated when *any* date is attached (windows are for `range`/`recurring` only), so the gate catches this class.
