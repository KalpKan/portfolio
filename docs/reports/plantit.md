## Defects

### D17 — When the day's Pl@ntNet budget is spent, a real photo is saved as a made-up species with "Confidence: 89 %"
Severity: **major** (S2/S5 honesty; the spec's letter, "Demo result only when `demo: true`", is met, but a stranger is told their aloe is a pothos, sees a confidence number Pl@ntNet never produced, and gets pothos watering targets saved to their collection)
Steps to reproduce: with `/api/health` `spend.plantnet.count` = 50 (this round: 2026-09-19 21:41 UTC, after the mug took call 50), sign in, Add Plant, drop `aloe-vera.jpg`, Identify.
Expected: "Identification is unavailable until 00:00 UTC (today's budget is used up); nothing was saved", or the photo saved with species "unknown, identify later".
Actual: `200 {demo: true, reason: "plantnet_daily_limit", candidates[0]: Epipremnum aureum, score: 0.89, savedPlant: {...}}` in 2 029 ms; the results page shows the green "Saved to your collection." banner, a calm blue "Demo result. Today's Pl@ntNet identification budget is used up…" note, the aloe field photo captioned "Epipremnum aureum · Golden pothos · Confidence: 89 %" and the pothos care guide; My Plants shows the card with a "Demo result" chip and pothos thresholds (`plantit-r3-desktop-results-daylimit-demo-production-2026-09-19.jpg`, `plantit-r3-daylimit-probe-production-2026-09-19.json`).
Likely cause: `backend/src/providers.js:67-70` (`identify`: `!slot.allowed` → `pickDemoPlant(buffer)` with the bundled plant's canned `score`), `backend/src/demoPlants.js:118-125` (a hash of the bytes picks one of six species), and `app.js` saving a demo answer like a real one; `frontend/src/components/PlantDetails.js:23-27` renders the demo reason as `severity="info"` and still prints "Confidence: 89 %".
Suggested fix: at the limit, answer `503 {unavailable: true, reason: "plantnet_daily_limit", retryAt: <next UTC midnight>}` and save nothing (keep the demo path only for `no_plantnet_key`, i.e. a deployment that never had a key); if a demo answer must stay, never print a confidence for it and do not auto-save it.

### D18 — A normal phone photo over 4 MB is refused with "use one under 4 MB" and no way to comply
Severity: **major** (S2 for a stranger with their own photo: modern phones write 3–10 MB JPEGs; the client already knows the server will shrink the image to 800 px)
Steps to reproduce: at 390 px, Add Plant → pick a 9 MB 6000×5000 JPEG (`scratchpad/at-limit-6000x5000.jpg`, production 2026-09-19 21:47 UTC).
Expected: the browser downscales the photo (canvas, longest side ≈ 1600 px) and identifies it; the size limit is invisible below ~25 MB.
Actual: "That image is too large. Please use one under 4 MB.", Identify disabled, no request (`plantit-r3-too-large-production-2026-09-19.json`, `plantit-r3-phone-too-large-production-2026-09-19.jpg`). A phone user has no resize tool; the only path is a screenshot of the photo.
Likely cause: `frontend/src/components/PlantUpload.js:21,44-46` (`MAX_IMAGE_SIZE = 4 MB` hard refusal); the server's `normaliseImage` (`app.js`, sharp → 800 px) already tolerates any input, only Vercel's 4.5 MB body limit (`MAX_UPLOAD_BYTES`) forces the client cap.
Suggested fix: in `onDrop`, when `picked.size > 1.5 MB`, decode with `createImageBitmap`/`<img>`, draw to a canvas with the longest side 1600 px, `toBlob('image/jpeg', 0.85)` and use that blob (keep the original as the preview); refuse only decode failures. Keep EXIF orientation via `createImageBitmap(file, { imageOrientation: 'from-image' })`.

## UX critique (impeccable `critique`)

## Known limitations that are NOT defects

- **Google account pick cannot be automated from this harness.** The chooser is a separate Chrome window outside the extension's tab group (real Chrome, this round: clicking the button loaded the Firebase auth iframe + `identitytoolkit …/projects` authorized-domain check and disabled the button as "Opening Google sign-in…"; the chooser then opened as its own window the extension cannot see; no app console message). The full round trip on this host was proven by the Phase 2–4 audit (real Chrome, `water_now_clicked` from `plantit.kalpkan.com`, 2026-09-18 23:26 UTC) and round 2 proved the cancel path. Every signed-in story here uses a session minted from the operator service account for throwaway uids (`round3-*`, `corpus-r3`, `corpus-test`) and written into IndexedDB.
- **The claude-in-chrome window is shared** with other agents' tabs (Plato, emotes) and is stuck at 606 × 627 px; `resize_window` reported success but the viewport did not change. Both widths were measured with Playwright Chromium 153 (1440×900 dark, 390×844 iPhone emulation, DPR 3, light scheme).
- **No webcam or video feature** exists in this app, so the Playwright fake-camera rig does not apply.
- **Dark theme only** (`mode: 'dark'` in `theme.js`; `body` background `rgb(16,17,18)` under `prefers-color-scheme: light` too), so "both themes" collapses to one.
- **Pl@ntNet budget**: the hosted app caps itself at 50 identifications per UTC day. It stood at 49/50 when this round started (21:26 UTC), so the live corpus + browser identifies were run after the 00:00 UTC reset; the day-limit state (D17) was measured on purpose with calls 50 and 51 of 2026-09-19. Kalp's Pl@ntNet account itself is at 500/day; this round used 18 (local stack) + 6 (local harness) + 1 (mug, production) + 16 (corpus, production) + 6 (production harness) = 47 of it.
- **A valid visitor OpenAI key was not tested** ($0 rule: no key to spend); the invalid-key path is proven end to end on production.
- **Hardware-required calls print the browser's own "Failed to load resource: 502 / 400 / 422"** lines; the app itself logs nothing.
- **A deleted photo keeps serving from Supabase's CDN briefly** (`cf-cache-status`); the object is gone from the bucket and the same URL answers `400` within minutes (three URLs deleted at 21:33 UTC answered 400 at 21:36 UTC, cache-busted too).
- **Photos added before 2026-09-18** (Firebase Storage on Spark) show the labelled "Photo no longer available" placeholder by design.
- **Real iPhone Safari** (popup sign-in with a `firebaseapp.com` authDomain under ITP) was not exercised; only Chromium's iPhone emulation.
