### D1 — Facing the other way, every bad rep is a good rep: the mirrored bad-form clips score 4/4, 4/4, 4/3 good and the worm reps "good", with a green skeleton and no hint

Severity: **blocker** (S3 is the story the product is named for and it fails silently for half of all set-ups; S10 discloses the limit but the page never tells the visitor which way to face)

Steps to reproduce: build a mirrored fake camera (`ffmpeg -i tests/fixtures/clips/bad_IMG_4456.mp4 … -vf hflip …`, exact recipe in the last section) and run `node docs/reports/evidence/pushups-r3-cam.mjs https://pushups.kalpkan.com/ out.json flip=<file>:7`; or lie down side-on with the head on the left of the raw frame (on a laptop: feet towards the right of the mirrored preview) and let the hips sag.

Expected / Actual: red skeleton, "hips sagging" / "keep your body straight", attempts up, good flat. Actual (`pushups-r3-cam-variants-2026-09-19.txt`): `flip-bad_IMG_4456` **4 attempts / 4 good**, `flip-bad_IMG_4451` **4 / 4**, `flip-bad_IMG_4470` 4 / 3 (one "hips sagging"), `flip-test_video` 4 / 3 (rep 1 "hips sagging", the two worm reps at 9.5 s and the clean reps all "good"; truth 0 0 1 1), `flip-demo` 4 / 4; "Good form" drawn on 158–234 frames per clip. Counting is unaffected (`flip-good_IMG_4409` 5/5). In the training orientation the same clips give 4/0, 4/0, 4/0, 4/2 in all three corpus runs.

Evidence: `pushups-r3-cam-variants-2026-09-19.{txt,json}`, screenshots `pushups-r3-cam-variants-shots/` not kept (final frames are black); compare `pushups-r3-corpus-live-run1-2026-09-19.log`.

Likely cause: `src/form.ts:106` `classifierBad = prob == null || !g.trainingOrientation ? null : prob <= 0.5` switches the classifier off whenever the feet are on the right of the raw frame, on the strength of a 2026-09-18 measurement that the *other person's* clean reps (IMG_1359, filmed the other way) scored 0.00; `src/formFeatures.ts` is mirror-invariant (`tests/formFeatures.test.ts` "flipping the frame horizontally gives the same features"), so the gate is keyed on the wrong variable: the classifier fails on a different body, not on a facing. With the classifier off, only `SAG_DEV = 0.18` (`form.ts:54`) is left for a sag, and on the browser's landmarks it fires on 1 of the 12 labelled sagging reps of `bad_IMG_4456/4451/4470` (in the training orientation the same reps are caught by the classifier and shown as "keep your body straight"; the Python traces put `bad_IMG_4470` at "hips sagging" ×4, so the geometry threshold is also sitting on the edge of the browser landmarks' noise). `index.html` lede says "side-on" and nothing about facing; the limitation is the fourth bullet of "How it decides".

Suggested fix: remove the orientation gate (or gate on a measured per-session confidence instead), then replay the corpus with mirrored features (x → 1 − x on the 36-vector) in `tests/corpus.test.ts` as a third trace set and add the five mirrored MJPEGs to `scripts/make-mjpeg.mjs` (a `mirror: true` flag) so `e2e-corpus.mjs` runs them; if the classifier really cannot be trusted for the other facing, then show a hint ("Turn around: form is only graded with your head on the right") and keep the count, never a silent "Good form". Re-tune `SAG_DEV` on the browser landmarks (`bad_IMG_4470` sits at the threshold) so geometry catches a plain sag on its own.

Likely cause: `src/classifier.ts` scores one frame at a time from 24 hip-centred coordinates and the ensemble does not remove the frame-to-frame swings; `tracker.ts:31-32` smooths the live verdict over only 0.25 s / 3 frames, which a signal alternating every frame defeats; `repCounter.ts:252-254` decides on a plain mean with a hard 0.5 threshold, so a rep whose mean is 0.50–0.55 lands on whichever frames were decoded.

Suggested fix: run the probability through an EMA (~0.4 s time constant, time-based) before both the live verdict and the rep mean; grade the rep on the smoothed value with a margin (< 0.4 bad, > 0.6 good, between: fall back to the geometry's sign or say "unsure" and count it as neither); draw the live verdict only from the smoothed value, and never red during a rep whose geometry is clean unless the smoothed value stays < 0.4 for 0.3 s. Then make the frame-drop test in `corpus.test.ts` require identical **good** counts too (today it exempts "coin flips"), and retire the "a rep it scores right at 50/50 can land either way" line from the page.

## Known limitations that are NOT defects

- `IMG_1305`'s first labelled rep (bottom 0.7 s) begins before the clip's first frame (shoulder already a third of the way down), so the pipeline reports it as a partial ("Go lower" at 0.8 s) and counts 5 of 6: inside the tolerance and consistent with the ground truth's own rule; the label could be `total: 5`. Not counted as a verdict miss above (65/68 includes it as a "None"; 65/67 without it).
- `IMG_1359`'s rep 6 (13.6 s) was relabelled high → medium by FIX r2 with a frame-level reason (hips 0.33 torso above the line); the r3 screenshot at 13.6 s (`pushups-r3-overlay-montage`) shows the hips raised, so the relabel is defensible. A label change by a fixer is nonetheless the kind of thing a reviewer should keep an eye on; this report checked it rather than trusting it.
- iOS Safari / Android Chrome on a real phone could not be driven from this Mac; S6's device half stays with H15. Mirroring, `facingMode: "user"`, width, portrait layout, 8 fps behaviour and rate independence were verified in headless Chrome and Chromium with fake cameras.
- Kalp's Chrome via claude-in-chrome is a hidden tab: `<video>` never reaches `loadedmetadata` there (Chrome defers media in hidden tabs), so the status stays at "Loading the pose model…" after Play; this is the browser, not the app (every visible-tab run works). The hidden tab was still useful for the load path and cache probe.
- The synthetic "frontal" camera shows an upper body only, so the feet-out hint legitimately outranks "Turn side-on"; a full-body frontal clip was not available. The `pausesCounting` path for a frontal pose is covered by `tests/hints.test.ts`.
- Light colour scheme: the site is dark-only by design (`color-scheme: dark`); both schemes render the identical page.
- The mirrored clips were made by flipping Kalp's recordings; MediaPipe's landmarks on a flipped image are not exactly a mirror of the originals, which is why `flip-bad_IMG_4470` still catches one sag by geometry while its original catches none. The conclusion (classifier off → sags pass) does not depend on that.
- PostHog session replay records DOM events, not canvas pixels (`recordCanvas` off), so "no frame leaves the device" holds.
