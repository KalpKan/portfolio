### D1 — 43 % of the corpus's bad reps are graded "good": `bad_IMG_4456` scores 4 attempts / 4 good with a green skeleton and "Rep N: good", and four worm ascents plus a pike pass too

Severity: **blocker** (S3 is the story the product is named for; its bar says the five bad clips yield 0 good and every high-confidence verdict matches, and the miss is silent: a green skeleton, "Good form", "Rep 2: good". It is honest on the page, which is why the score still rose, but a visitor who sags on purpose to test the grader sees it praised four times. Steps to reproduce: `GPU=1 node scripts/e2e-corpus.mjs https://pushups.kalpkan.com/ bad_IMG_4456` (or `MIRROR=1`), or `node docs/reports/evidence/pushups-r4-shots.mjs https://pushups.kalpkan.com/ /tmp/shots bad_IMG_4456=tests/fixtures/clips/.mjpeg/bad_IMG_4456.mjpeg:1.9,2.6,3.0,4.4` and look at the frames; or lie down, drop the hips to the floor, arch the lower back with the head up and push the chest up first (the training folder's "bad" style).

Actual, identical on every run in both facings: `bad_IMG_4456` **4 / 4** (`Rep 1: good` … `Rep 4: good`, "Good form" on every frame: `pushups-r4-overlay-montage-2026-09-20.jpg` top row, tiles 2/2 at 2.6 s and 3.0 s), demo **4 / 4** ("Clip finished: 4 good of 4"), `test_video3` 5/5 (truth 2–4 good), `test_video` 4/3 (truth 2), `test_video_4` 5/5 (truth 4), `IMG_1513` 1/1 (truth 0). High-confidence verdicts 58/68 live (57/68 mirrored); of the 21 high-confidence **bad** reps, 9 are "good" (12/21 = 57 % of bad reps caught), of the 47 high-confidence good reps, 46 are "good" (the one miss is IMG_1305's first rep, a limitation).

Evidence: `pushups-r4-corpus-live-x3-2026-09-20.log`, `pushups-r4-verdicts-live-run1-2026-09-20.txt`, `pushups-r4-verdicts-live-mirrored-2026-09-20.txt`, `pushups-r4-overlay-montage-2026-09-20.jpg`, `pushups-r4-demo-run1-2026-09-20.json`, `pushups-r4-ascent-features-2026-09-20.{txt,json}` (the analysis below).

Likely cause: `src/form.ts` judges a rep on four per-frame rules (hip deviation ±0.18/−0.26 torso, knee angle < 130°, body angle) at the top and the bottom and two bottom-window means (`elbowAhead > −0.09` "dropped to the floor", `wristBelow > 0.5 && hipBelowShoulder > 0.2` chest-up sag), `repCounter.ts:239-253`; nothing judges the **ascent**, which is where a worm shows (chest up before the hips), and `bad_IMG_4456` sits inside every rule's clean range (bottom-window mean `elbowAhead` −0.35…−0.39, `wristBelow` 0.32–0.36, max `hipDev` on the way up 0.07–0.11 against `SAG_DEV` 0.18). I re-measured this round on all 84 labelled reps (browser set) rather than take FIX r3's word: the hip-lag ratio at 40 % shoulder rise is 0.40–0.62 on the four `bad_IMG_4456` reps and 0.43–0.96 on clean reps (clean reps from `IMG_1359`/`IMG_1512` sit at 0.43–0.53), the max ascent `hipDev` is 0.07–0.14 on the worm reps and 0.09–0.15 on the clean reps of `IMG_1360` (low, close phone), so the measures the fixer tried genuinely overlap; the one measure I could not test is the head: in the `bad_IMG_4456` frames the head is thrown up (looking at the wall, nose well above the shoulders) while every clean bottom keeps the neck neutral, and `features.ts` throws the 11 face landmarks away before the rules see them.

Suggested fix (1–3 sentences): add an ascent window to the rep verdict (frames from `bottomT` to the count), and give it two rules the current features cannot express: (a) the shoulder–hip line's angle relative to the hip–ankle line at the moment the shoulders are 25–30 % up (a worm bends at the hip early; a rigid plank does not), measured at fixed shoulder-rise fractions so it is frame-rate independent, and (b) a neck/head cue (nose above the shoulder line by more than ~0.3 torso at the bottom or early ascent = "head up, hips down"), which needs the nose landmark passed through `features.ts`; tune both on the three trace sets with `IMG_1360`'s close-phone reps as the hardest clean case and keep them behind the same majority. If after that `bad_IMG_4456` still passes, change the product promise instead: rename the counter's "good" to "no fault seen" on the overlay and stop the demo caption from claiming 2–3 clean.

- `IMG_1359`'s rep 6 (13.6 s) was relabelled high → medium by FIX r2 with a frame-level reason; it is graded "hips too high" and the 13.6 s screenshot shows the hips raised; defensible.
- iOS Safari / Android Chrome on a real phone could not be driven from this Mac; S6's device half stays with H15. Mirroring, `facingMode: "user"`, width, portrait layout, 6–11 fps behaviour and rate independence were verified in headless Chrome and Chromium with fake cameras.
- Kalp's Chrome via claude-in-chrome is a hidden tab: `<video>` never reaches `loadedmetadata` there, so the demo does not play; the tab was used for the load path and the cache probe only. My r3-style camera runner also timed out twice at a 390 px viewport with the Mac UA + `?trace`, while the r2 UX script's portrait check (iPhone UA) and a plain 390 px run both worked; harness flakiness, not the app (the phone path is verified by those two).
- The synthetic "frontal" camera shows an upper body only, so the feet-out hint legitimately outranks "Turn side-on"; the frontal pause path is covered by `tests/hints.test.ts`.
- Light colour scheme: the site is dark-only by design (`color-scheme: dark`); both schemes render the identical page (verified at four widths).
- The mirrored clips were made by flipping Kalp's recordings; MediaPipe's landmarks on a flipped image are not an exact mirror of the originals (D2/D3 are on those landmarks), so a real left-facing visitor may land slightly differently; the direction of the finding (a threshold with no dead band) does not depend on it.
- PostHog session replay records DOM events, not canvas pixels (`recordCanvas` off), so "no frame leaves the device" holds; the replay POSTs (6–15 KB) are the DOM.
- My re-measurement of the 2D worm signal (D1) is a first pass over five candidate measures on the 12-landmark feature vector; it confirms the fixer's overlap claim for those five and does not rule out the head cue or a rigid-body angle rule, which are the suggested next attempts.
