# Project descriptions, in Kalp's own words (dictated 2026-09-20)

These are the source of truth for every project's tagline, case-study "problem" paragraph, terminal README, and About text. Keep his voice (first person is fine on case-study pages: "I built…"). Do not invent facts beyond these plus what the repos show.

- **promptflip** — "Just a fun idea I came up with because I wanted to versus my friends in something, so me and my friends can have some fun." (Two players, each with their own OpenRouter key; a provably fair coin flip decides whose prompt gets answered and whose key pays.)
- **Basketball Stat Tracker (hoops)** — "I have a mini hoop in my room and I wanted to track my statistics any time I shot the ball. Set up the app and it counts makes and misses, longest streak, simple statistics like that. Just for myself."
- **Plato** — "I was tired of manually extracting assignments and deadlines and making study plans from the course outlines at the start of each semester. So I built one where I download all the course outlines, give them to it, it extracts everything and makes one calendar file that I drag and drop into my calendar."
- **Plant It** — "One of the projects I made with Yash, my friend. An automatic plant-watering system that does more than normal ones: you take a picture of your plant, upload it, we identify the species and get care instructions for it, for example what soil moisture it needs. Those species-specific numbers are fed into a microcontroller so each plant is watered to its own optimal level."
- **Pushup Form Tracker** — "My first project working with AI. I wanted to really learn the process of training a model myself. Me and Yash gathered the data ourselves and trained a model on data we collected ourselves."
- **Emote Detector** — "I love playing Clash Royale and wanted to work more with computer vision." (Webcam gestures fire Clash Royale emotes with sound.)
- **Microtubule Quantification** — "I needed to do a project for a class and there was no industry-standard way I was told of to quantify microtubules from immunofluorescence microscopy. So I built one."
- **UnPark (codename Antifreeze)** — "Built for a competition. It helps people with Parkinson's who experience freezing-of-gait episodes. The device is a haptic device worn on the ankle that detects freeze-of-gait episodes with an accelerometer. When it detects one it automatically logs it and sends it to your phone (auto-logging). On the phone the data populates a dashboard, and an AI model runs in the background deriving insights people never thought about. You can export all the collected data so monitoring disease progression is easier."
- **Automatic RC Car** — "One of my hardware projects: a small RC car we built that is controlled by a PS4 controller, but can be switched to drive itself. We loaded a computer-vision model that detects tennis balls onto the Raspberry Pi on the car, and the car follows any green tennis ball you throw."
- **FlashCards** — under construction; nothing to write yet.
- **Porsche PCB keychain** — "Made for my friend Yash's birthday, one of my first PCB-design projects. A small PCB with a hole so you can attach it to a keychain, with a Porsche cutout. Three LEDs on the underside and a USB-C port: plug it into your phone and the LEDs light up through the cutout so a Porsche glows."
